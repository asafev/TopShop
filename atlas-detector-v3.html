Methodology & Signal Explanations

All 100+ signals run client-side with zero external dependencies. Signals are organized in 12 categories. Each probes a different browser subsystem that Atlas (OpenAI's Chromium-based browsing tool, Mac-only, cloud-hosted) may expose differences in. The system fingerprints the environment rather than relying on easily-patched property checks.

Research sources: bot.sannysoft.com, browserleaks.com (canvas/webgl/css/fonts/features), fingerprint.com techniques, CreepJS, FingerprintJS, Antoine Vastel's fpscanner, pixelscan.net, Chromium source, W3C specs, MDN Web Docs, PerimeterX decoded signatures, intoli.com headless tests, arh.antoinevastel.com, deviceandbrowserinfo.com, and codebase detectors (200+ signals cataloged).
